NGO Website Essential Features Design Presentation (Task 5)
A visually engaging breakdown of the features required to maximize impact, donations, and volunteer engagement.

Feature 01

Hero Section & Quick Donation

The Hero section captures immediate attention with an emotional, high-quality image of your work. By placing a clear Call-To-Action (CTA) right at the top, visitors can instantly navigate to the donation portal, increasing conversion rates for the foundation.

Impact Showcase

Feature 03

Campaigns & Events

Highlighting ongoing campaigns creates a sense of urgency. Using progress bars helps supporters see how close a campaign is to its goal, motivating them to contribute and share the event on social media.

Feature 04

Volunteer Form

A streamlined, accessible volunteer registration form removes friction for those wanting to help. Placing it alongside an inspiring message encourages user engagement and grows your on-the-ground support network.

Become a Changemaker

Feature 05

Testimonials & Social Proof

Sharing real stories from volunteers, sponsors, or beneficiaries adds a human touch. Testimonials serve as powerful social proof, validating the NGO’s impact and encouraging others to get involved.

Feature 06

Contact & Support Section

A clear Contact & Support footer is vital for an NGO. It ensures that media, potential major sponsors, and volunteers can easily find your physical location, direct email, and social links without digging through the site.
